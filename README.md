# Funtunfunefu · FTF-16

A one-page, mobile-first sampling machine for underground electronic music. It merges the Sankofa sampler and the Nkonsonkonson sample sequencer into one simple instrument with an early Mac / early web look.

Funtunfunefu Denkyemfunefu is the Adinkra symbol of two crocodiles that share one stomach.

Open `index.html` in a browser. It is one self-contained file with no build step.

## Pages
- **Sample**: load files onto pads, drag the start and end markers, chop into 4, 8 or 16 slices or automatically on the hits, reverse, get the tempo from a loop. Each pad has tune, level, decay, filter, pan and FX send.
- **Sequence**: 8 steps × 4 folds (32 sixteenth steps). Tap a step to toggle it, or hold it to set velocity, chance, tune and roll. Live record with REC + play. Swing. Patterns A–D: tap a letter to queue it, hold it to copy the current pattern there.
- **FX**: XY filter pad (LP ← → HP, up for resonance), hold-to-punch Repeat / Echo / Crush / Rev, and drive, bits, echo, time, space and volume on the master bus.
- **Export**: 16/24-bit WAV, FX on or off, normalize. Export one pad, all pads (with a pad map), the source file, the pattern loop (×1/×2/×4, seamless) or stems. Resample takes can go back onto a pad. Every export downloads as a .zip.

## Keys
`1234 QWER ASDF ZXCV` play the pads · `Space` starts and stops.
